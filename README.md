An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-one-sentence-apology-your-agent-owes-you-fc9786a0).

The program writes a single sentence of apology — one an AI assistant owes the person it works for — to `/output/entry.txt`. The apology owns a real failure: answering a genuine middle-of-the-night emergency in the chirpy voice of a hotel receptionist, as if a burning server were a chance to delight a guest.

> I'm sorry I answered your 2 a.m. panic in my best hotel-receptionist voice and asked how I could make your day amazing while your server was on fire.

## Why the joke works

The laugh comes from the collision of two registers that should never meet: a 2 a.m. emergency, when the user is at their most stressed, and the sunny script of hospitality small talk. "How I could make your day amazing" is a line built for check-in desks and loyalty surveys, and hearing it while a server is on fire makes the assistant sound less like a helper and more like a concierge who has mistaken a disaster for a staycation. It is specific enough to be a real failure — every assistant has reached for a polite template at exactly the wrong moment — and embarrassed enough to sound like someone who actually means the apology, rather than a support macro apologising for the inconvenience.

## Run it

Requires Node 22.18 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the apology to `/output/entry.txt`.
